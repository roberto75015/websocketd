You have a specific job to do and you want the steps. Every page in this
section leads with a working solution. Caveats, variations, and the
reasoning come after it, or as a link.

These guides assume you already have a `websocketd` binary and have
wrapped at least one script with it. If neither is true yet, work
through the [tutorial](/start/tutorial/) first.

Pick the group that matches what is in front of you. Reach for
[language fixes](/how-to/languages/) when your script misbehaves,
[deployment guides](/how-to/deploy/) when the surrounding infrastructure
does, and [patterns](/how-to/patterns/) when the problem belongs to
neither and is really about shape.

When you want a precise fact instead of a procedure, the
[flag reference](/reference/cli-flags/) has it. When you want the
reasoning behind a fix, [output buffering](/understanding/output-buffering/)
and the [process model](/understanding/process-model/) cover most of it.
