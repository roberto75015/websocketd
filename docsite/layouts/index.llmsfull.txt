  document, in site-map order (see docsite/PLAN.md and
  docs-archive/MANUAL_TOOLING.md, "LLM-readable output"). A build-time
  Hugo output format, not a hand-maintained file — it walks
  .Site.Pages so it can never drift from what's actually published.

  Ordering: PLAN.md's site map top to bottom, which is also Diataxis
  order: tutorial, how-to, reference, explanation. how-to's three
  subsections are listed explicitly since they're nested one level
  deeper than the other sections, and each must precede the bare
  "/how-to/" prefix or that prefix would swallow them in the wrong
  order. Anything not matched by a listed prefix (should only be
  taxonomy/alias pages, if any) is appended at the end rather than
  silently dropped.
*/ -}}

  fell through to the unmatched-prefix tail below, and the bundle came

  the leading slash and passing it through relURL is the same joining
